How it works

  1. ⚡

    1. Sprint

    Ten questions against the clock at the child's class and level: Simple, Medium, Hard or Pro. Fast, correct answers earn bonus points.

  2. 💡

    2. Learn from mistakes

    A wrong answer pauses the game and shows the working step by step, like a teacher at the board. It can even be read aloud.

  3. 🏆

    3. Climb and compete

    Weekly leagues with classmates, races against friends, a Boss Battle every week and badges to collect.

Safe for children. Honest with parents.

BrainSprint is a learning game, not a gambling one. We designed it so children can't spend money or be contacted by strangers.

  • ✅ No ads, ever.
  • ✅ Children never buy anything. Points are only earned by answering correctly.
  • ✅ Friends only by code, shared in person. No searching for strangers, no chat.
  • ✅ First names or nicknames only. No photos, phone numbers or locations are shown.
  • ✅ Grown-up areas are locked behind a maths question and a parent PIN.
  • ✅ Brain Power builds healthy breaks into play, and it refills for free.

Questions parents ask

How much does it cost?

It's free while we build it. Later, a free daily amount will stay, and parents will be able to buy a day, week or month pass with MTN MoMo or Airtel Money for unlimited play.

Is it really the Ugandan syllabus?

The Maths topics follow the primary syllabus class by class, from counting in Top Class to PLE topics in P7, such as number bases, finite systems, sets and simple interest.

What is Brain Power?

Each wrong answer uses one and they refill for free over time. It encourages careful thinking over random tapping, and builds in natural breaks.

Can strangers contact my child?

No. There is no chat, and friends can only be added with a code your child shares in person. Other players see only a first name or nickname, an animal picture and game scores.

How do I follow my child's progress?

In the app, tap Grown-ups, answer the grown-up question, and create a free account with your phone number and a PIN. You'll see what they practised, how accurate they were and their league position.

Does it use a lot of data?

No. The game is under half a megabyte, works offline once opened, and only sends small score updates.
